Squeezed fresh. Never rushed.

At Thropican, every glass starts with the fruit, not a formula. We choose ripe mango, pineapple, orange, papaya, passion fruit and more, then prepare them by hand right at our stand.

No preservatives, no colorings, nothing hiding in the cup. Just real fruit, blended fresh and poured into a cold 400 ml glass, so a simple juice feels like a little tropical getaway.

  1. 🧺 PickRipe, selected fruit chosen for true-to-nature flavor.
  2. 🔪 PrepareWashed, peeled and cut by hand at the stand.
  3. 🥭 BlendSqueezed or blended fresh when you order.
  4. 🧊 ServePoured cold into 400 ml, with no preservatives or colorings.
  5. 🚚 DeliverBrought fresh to your door, just as cold and fruity.
